# Brief — /hype

**What it is:** an agent skill that turns whatever you want to promote into ready-to-post social videos.
**For whom:** indie makers, developers and small teams who ship things but don't have a marketer or a video editor.
**The pain or desire:** you launch, post a screenshot, and it disappears in the feed. Making a real video takes a day you don't have.
**The promise:** type one line, get ideas that fit, get finished videos in every size with captions.
**Why this, not the alternatives:** it reads your actual project (code, site, colors) instead of filling a generic template, and it pitches ideas first.
**Proof we can use:** it's free and open source; this video was made with it. No usage numbers yet — don't invent any.
**Goal of the campaign:** awareness + GitHub stars.
**Call to action:** github.com/AliMi00/hype
**Voice & tone:** confident, a bit cheeky, maker-to-maker. Avoid "AI-powered", "revolutionary".
**Visual identity:** dark (#0c0a14), hot pink (#ff3d71), sun yellow (#ffc53d), violet (#8b5cf6); Space Grotesk + JetBrains Mono.
**Platforms & formats:** X, LinkedIn, TikTok/Reels/Shorts → 9:16, 1:1, 16:9.
**Material available:** the terminal flow, the idea list format, the aspect ratios.
**Constraints:** ≤ 16s. Works on mute.
**Assumptions:** the audience already knows what an agent/CLI is.
