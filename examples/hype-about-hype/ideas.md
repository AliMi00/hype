**Format:** Pain → fix · **Platform:** X, LinkedIn, TikTok · **Aspect:** 9:16, 1:1, 16:9 · **Length:** 16s · **Tone:** bold
**Hook (0–2s):** "You shipped it." then, in pink, "Nobody saw it."
**Beats:** 1) Type one line in the terminal 2) Ideas that fit, pick one 3) Every size → CTA "github.com/AliMi00/hype"
**Why it works:** every maker has felt the silent launch; the fix is shown in the same 16 seconds.
